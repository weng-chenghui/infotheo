The chapter `chapters/ssprove.tex` uses the impersonal third-person
"this thesis" consistently for contribution and intention statements
(lines 3, 9, 133, 157, 163). This is the author's deliberate style
choice for this chapter; it is not a voice error.

**Why:** The author self-references via "this thesis" rather than "I"
throughout the chapter, which is an acceptable impersonal variant.
The artifact-"our" at line 35 ("In our translation") is the standard
exempt artifact label.

**How to apply:** In future runs on this chapter (or chapters with the
same "this thesis" pattern), do not flag "this thesis" constructions.
Do watch for "our X enables / contributes / differs / achieves" in
related-work or conclusion paragraphs where surrounding prose uses
"this thesis" — the mismatch is the tell for V2/V3. The one confirmed
lapse is line 158: "our use of \ssprove{} enables a key difference"
(flagged as voice-ssprove-1, Major, V2).
