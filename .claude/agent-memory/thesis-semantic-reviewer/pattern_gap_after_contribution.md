In `chapters/interpreter.tex` §1 (Motivation), the contribution sentence
("we obtain [the framework] by embedding...") appears before the gap sentence
("No existing dedicated tool unifies these layers."). This inverts the
E3 gap-before-contribution structure. The same contribution is also stated in
the first paragraph, making the second paragraph's contribution clause doubly
redundant.

**Why:** The second paragraph was drafted to explain the tool-choice rationale
after the embedding strategy was already announced, so the gap justification
was added as an afterthought at the end.

**How to apply:** In motivation/introduction sections, always check that the
gap or limitation sentence precedes any contribution sentence in the same
paragraph. If the contribution was already stated in a prior paragraph,
the current paragraph should open with the gap and let the contribution
follow as its resolution.
