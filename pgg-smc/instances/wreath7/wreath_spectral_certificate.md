# Wreath Z_7 wr S_2 Schreier spectral certificate

Companion note for `wreath_spectral_certificate.py` and `wreath_mixing.v`.
This is the Z_7 wr S_2 analogue of `s5_spectral_certificate.{py,md}`.

## What is certified

The wreath plug's anonymity rests on the mixing of a random walk on the 14
card positions. At each step the walk applies a uniformly random element of the
inverse-closed generator multiset

    wreath_sym_gens = [cut1; cut1^-1; cut2; cut2^-1; wswap]

(cut1, cut2 are the two within-pile 7-cycles; wswap is the pile swap). The
walk's transition matrix `Q` is 14x14, symmetric, and doubly stochastic.

The certificate establishes the **Rayleigh bound on `Q^2`**:

    for every mean-zero v in R^14,   <v, Q^2 v>  <=  alpha^2 * <v, v>,

with `alpha = 17/20`. This is exactly the Rocq `Axiom wreath_rayleigh_Qsq_R`,
the single imported assumption of `wreath_mixing.v`. Everything else in the
spectral-to-variation-distance chain is a structural Rocq proof.

## Why the inverses are needed

The S_5 mixing engine `symm_ds_TV_bound` derives symmetry of the Schreier
transition from per-generator **involutivity** (g^2 = 1). The wreath cuts
`cut1`, `cut2` are 7-cycles, not involutions, so the bare 3-generator
transition `[cut1; cut2; wswap]` is **not symmetric**. Z_7 has odd order, so the
base group cannot be generated by involutions at all. Adjoining the inverses
makes the generating multiset closed under inverse, which makes `Q` symmetric
without changing the generated group `<<wreath_sym_gens>> = Z_7 wr S_2`.

## The spectral value

`Q` commutes with the pile-swap involution, so its spectrum splits into a
pile-symmetric and a pile-antisymmetric sector, each a 7-cycle circulant:

  - symmetric sector eigenvalues:     3/5 + (2/5) cos(2 pi k / 7),  k = 0..6;
  - antisymmetric sector eigenvalues: 1/5 + (2/5) cos(2 pi k / 7),  k = 0..6.

The stationary eigenvalue is the symmetric `k = 0` value, `1`. The
second-largest eigenvalue modulus is the symmetric `k = 1` value,

    lambda_2 = 3/5 + (2/5) cos(2 pi / 7) ~ 0.84940.

Since `cos(2 pi / 7) < 5/8`, the rational `alpha = 17/20 = 0.85` is a strict
upper bound, giving spectral gap `1 - alpha = 3/20 = 0.15`. This is wider than
the S_5 gap `19/200 ~ 0.095`: the wreath mixes faster.

## How it is certified (exact, not numerical)

`wreath_spectral_certificate.py` builds `Q` over exact rationals, checks it is
symmetric and doubly stochastic, forms `M = alpha^2 I - Q^2`, restricts to the
13-dimensional mean-zero hyperplane through the differences basis
`B` (column j = e_j - e_{j+1}), and computes an exact-rational LDL^T
factorisation of the 13x13 Gram matrix `S = B^T M B`. All 13 pivots are
strictly positive, witnessing `S` positive definite, hence the Rayleigh bound.
The reconstruction `L D L^T = S` is checked exactly over Q.

## Trust model

Only the Rayleigh conclusion enters Rocq, exactly as for `s5_rayleigh_Qsq_R`.
No rational constant from the LDL^T factorisation is referenced by the Rocq
proof. The statement is provable in Rocq by entrywise sum-of-squares expansion;
it is imported here because tactic-level rational normalisation at these
coefficient sizes exceeds a practical budget.

Re-run `python3 wreath_spectral_certificate.py` whenever `alpha` or `Q`
changes; do not hand-edit `wreath_alpha_R` in `wreath_mixing.v`.
