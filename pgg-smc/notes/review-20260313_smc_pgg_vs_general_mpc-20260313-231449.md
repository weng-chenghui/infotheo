### False Claims

| ID | Location | Claim | Why False |
|----|----------|-------|-----------|
| F1 | §5 Claim 3 | "The entropy of the adversary's uncertainty is log₂\|L_g\|" | Holds only under uniform distribution over L_g; uniformity assumption is unstated. The protocol must be *proven* to induce uniformity. |
| F2 | §8.4 | "A designer using SMC-PGG knows all of these things from (G, I, ℓ)" — specifically "what security margin they have" | "Security margin" is not formally defined or provably derivable from (G, I, ℓ). No function from these parameters to a security bound is established anywhere. |
| F3 | §1.3 | "aperiodic layers can be handled by threshold broadcast" | No construction, plausibility argument, or protocol sketch is given. Aperiodic automata can compute non-trivial functions; the threshold broadcast mapping is a non-trivial protocol design problem. *(Opus override: was Suspicious)* |
| F4 | §5 Claim 2 | "Full commutativity (I = Σ × Σ): Foata depth 1 (non-interactive)" | In MPC terminology, "non-interactive" = 0 rounds (no communication). Foata depth 1 = 1 round (simultaneous broadcast). These are distinct complexity classes. *(Opus override: was Suspicious)* |
| F5 | §5 Claim 2 | "one can have I = Σ × Σ with G = S_5, achieving Foata depth 1 and NC^1" | Mathematical contradiction: I = Σ × Σ forces all generators to commute in the trace monoid. The image in G is generated by pairwise-commuting elements, producing an *abelian* subgroup. S_5 is non-abelian, so NC^1 (requiring non-solvable G) is not achievable with full commutativity. The paper's own concrete demonstration (Ex. 3) contradicts this claim. *(Opus override: was Suspicious)* |
| F6 | §8.4 | Garbled circuit designers don't know "how the protocol composes with other protocols" | UC framework (Canetti 2001, Lindell-Pinkas 2009) provides precise composition theorems for garbled circuits. This misrepresents the state of the art. *(Opus override: was Suspicious)* |

### Suspicious Claims

| ID | Location | Claim | Concerns |
|----|----------|-------|----------|
| S1 | §1.1 | Fiber size "computable from the group's Cayley graph" | Requires Cayley graph *and* word length ℓ; \|L_g\| = (A^ℓ)_{id,g} where A is adjacency matrix. Misleadingly stated. |

## 3. Academic Writing Critique

1. **No explicit contribution statement.** The paper has no "Contributions" paragraph or bulleted list identifying what is new vs expository. The contributions taxonomy (established/extensions/conjectural) is helpful but is a classification of claims, not a statement of what the paper itself contributes. *Action: Add a paragraph stating what the paper establishes.*

2. **"Honest analysis" framing is not an academic genre.** The title signals an opinion piece, not a research contribution. *Action: Retitle to name the technical content; move the honest-vs-hype structure to a discussion section.*

3. **No prior-work section.** 10 references is thin for a paper spanning MPC, complexity theory, algebraic automata, and AG codes. Missing: Canetti (2001) UC framework, BMR (1990) N-party constant-round, Barthe et al. EasyCrypt formalizations, Barkol-Ishai NC^1 MPC, Tsfasman-Vlăduț-Zink AG codes, Eilenberg (1976) automata varieties. *Action: Add a dedicated Related Work section.*

4. **No formal definitions.** Trace monoid, Foata depth, fiber L_g, RAAG, monodromy group — all used without definition. Inaccessible to MPC audience without companion notes. *Action: Add a Preliminaries section with boxed definitions.*

5. **§2 and §3 are redundant.** Both cover NC^1 boundary. *Action: Merge into a single "Complexity Background" section.*

6. **§7 and §8 are logically inverted.** Negatives before positives buries the contribution. *Action: Present advantages first, then limitations.*

7. **Communication comparison not apples-to-apples.** Table uses different units (ℓ vs |C|, N vs n) without reconciliation. *Action: Add table footnote defining variables and expressing ℓ in terms of |C|.*

8. **"Honest caveat" label pattern is informal** — reads as blog post, not academic paper. *Action: Replace with numbered Remarks or Observations.*

9. **SMC-PGG adversary model underspecified.** Corruption threshold, party count, adaptive vs static not stated. *Action: Add to §4 table or as a standing assumption.*

10. **REVIEW-FIX HTML comments remain.** 23 `<!-- REVIEW-FIX: ... -->` comments from prior revision still in source. *Action: Strip all after verifying corresponding fixes.*

11. **§9 "Broader Lesson" has no theorem to generalize.** Reads as editorial without formal results preceding it. *Action: Reframe as open problems section with precise conjectures.*

12. **UC framework dismissed without engagement.** §8.4 claims garbled circuits lack composition guarantees, which contradicts Canetti (2001). *Action: Cite UC and state the algebraic-vs-circuit distinction precisely.*

## 6. Summary Verdict

The paper's mathematical foundations in complexity theory are largely sound (Barrington-Thérien, BGW thresholds, encoding blowup analysis all correct), but its protocol-level claims are significantly underdeveloped. Six false claims were identified, the most damaging being the mathematical contradiction that full commutativity (I = Σ×Σ) is compatible with G = S_5 (it forces an abelian subgroup), the misrepresentation of UC composition guarantees for garbled circuits, and the unstated uniformity assumption in the entropy formula. The paper reads as a well-informed technical blog post (formality distance 3.5/5) rather than a submission-ready paper: it lacks formal definitions, a prior-work section, and formal results (theorems with proofs). Major revision is required before venue submission.
