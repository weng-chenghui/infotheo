## Recommendation

1. **Primary**: Submit to **New Generation Computing** as a regular article (or wait for 4th special issue call). Frame as "spectral security bounds for multi-shuffle card protocols" — mathematical contribution with mechanized proofs as evidence.
2. **Simultaneous**: Post to **IACR ePrint** for immediate visibility.
3. **Conference backup**: Target **ASIACRYPT 2026** (~May deadline) or **STACS 2027** (~Sep deadline) for conference publication.
4. **AI disclosure**: Document AI assistance in Methods section per Springer policy. Frame as "AI-assisted formalization verified by human expert."
